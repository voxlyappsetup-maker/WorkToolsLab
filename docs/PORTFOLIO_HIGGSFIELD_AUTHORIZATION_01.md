## WorkToolsLab authorized use cases

Higgsfield AI is approved shared creative production infrastructure where it materially reduces cost/time, raises quality, removes a blocker, improves testing/acquisition, or creates reusable media.

Authorized examples include:

- product/project explainers;
- marketing visuals and motion assets;
- social/video content;
- tool demonstration concepts;
- thumbnails/covers;
- storyboards and visual prototypes;
- campaign variants;
- localization variants;
- presentation assets and reusable media.

## Authorized without a new owner gate

- evaluation;
- prompt development;
- storyboard/workflow design;
- concept/prototype generation;
- creative testing;
- technical workflow design;
- use of official ChatGPT/Claude/Higgsfield integrations;
- production using credits/subscription already approved for the portfolio.

## Separate owner approval still required

- new paid subscription;
- subscription-tier change;
- additional credit-pack purchase;
- annual commitment;
- unbounded API spend;
- public publication when no existing publish authorization covers it;
- paid campaign launch;
- ad spend.

## Quality gate

Higgsfield output is never automatically publish-ready. Applicable QA must cover accuracy, brand consistency, Arabic/RTL, rendered text, product fidelity, screenshots/logos, human anatomy, lip sync/audio, copyright/licensing, platform policy, AI disclosure, and final aspect ratio/resolution.
